## 🎨 Design Philosophy Changes

### **Before: Impressive & Flashy**
- Goal: Wow visitors with 3D effects
- Style: Gaming/tech demo aesthetic
- Colors: Neon cyan and purple
- Effects: Heavy animations and particles
- Target: Tech enthusiasts
- Vibe: "Look what I can build!"

### **After: Professional & Clean**
- Goal: Present work professionally
- Style: Modern SaaS aesthetic
- Colors: Professional navy and blue
- Effects: Subtle fade-ins only
- Target: Recruiters and hiring managers
- Vibe: "I'm a professional developer"
